# Conclusion

The journey, beginning with one simple and elegant idea -- building programs exclusively from pure,
composable functions -- has reached its logical end.
We have traveled from foundational principles to building real, practical systems.
This initial premise, despite its apparent simplicity, turned out to be incredibly powerful.
It served as a compass that consistently and principled guided through all aspects of program design,
opening an entirely new world of problem-solving approaches.

We explored how composition allows us to build complex systems from simple, verifiable, and reliable parts.
In the last chapters we made, perhaps, the most important step: we proved that these principles are not confined to a sterile academic world.
We built a full-fledged library for stream processing and incremental I/O,
demonstrating that even the "messiest" aspects of programming -- interaction with files,
network and user -- can be modeled purely functionally, while preserving our compositional, declarative style.
We erased the boundary between the "pure" core of the program and the "impure" outside world, without compromising principles.

Functional programming is not just a set of techniques; it is an infinitely deep topic and a new way of thinking.
Now you have a solid foundation -- not just tools, but an understanding of *why* they work.
This is enough to confidently continue the journey independently,
making FP not a weekend hobby, but an integral part of your professional work.

At first, applying these principles may require extra effort. Good design is always complex.
But over time, functional thinking will become your second nature.
You will begin to see opportunities for composition where you once saw only imperative instructions.
You will discover new, more powerful abstractions and patterns that solve seemingly insurmountable problems.

Go and apply this knowledge. Rewrite not only new code, but also rethink old code.
Share these ideas with your colleagues, debate, find better solutions.
Turn complexity into simplicity, chaos into order.

This journey is endlessly fascinating.
Enjoy it, keep learning, and may luck accompany you in all your future projects!
