# Translations and Problem Solutions

This section is devoted to Russian translations of articles, educational materials
and talks on functional programming in Scala.
It collects works covering topics such as:

- Functional programming
- Practical application of Scala (Cats Effect, fs2, ZIO)
- Optimization and application architecture

It also presents an extensive collection of solutions to various exercises, problems and puzzles
taken from various textbooks, problem books,
as well as from practical assignments offered in courses and online resources.

The goal of the section is to make quality materials available to the Russian-speaking audience, preserving the accuracy of terminology and the meaning of the originals.
