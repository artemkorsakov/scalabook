| [**Introduction to Functional Programming**](https://archive.org/details/introductiontofu0000bird/mode/2up)                                                                   | 🇬🇧 EN  | 1988    | @:note(🔍 More) Richard Bird's **Introduction to Functional Programming** is one of the first classic guides to functional programming, published in 1988. The book provides a thorough introduction to the functional paradigm, covering important aspects of the Miranda language (a predecessor of Haskell). The author thoroughly explains basic constructs of functional languages such as currying, function composition, recursion, and pattern matching. The book is especially valued for its clear exposition and mathematical rigor, backed by numerous exercises. While many examples are now outdated, the overall concept of functional style remains unchanged, and the book continues to be a useful source for learning the fundamentals of this important programming area. @:@ |
